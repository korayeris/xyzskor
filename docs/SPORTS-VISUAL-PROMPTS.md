# XYZSKOR spor görsel üretim promptları

Bu metinler XYZSKOR'un koyu lacivert, teknik, modern spor yayını kimliği için
hazırlanmış production promptlarıdır. Her görsel yerel asset olarak saklanmalı;
üçüncü taraf sitelerden hotlink yapılmamalıdır.

## Ortak üretim kuralları

- Ana çıktı: `16:9`, tercihen `1792×1024` veya daha büyük.
- Mobil güvenli kırpma: önemli özne merkezdeki yüzde 55'lik alanda kalmalı.
- Sol alt bölgede arayüz başlığı için sakin ve okunabilir negatif alan bırakılmalı.
- İnsanlar tamamen kurgusal olmalı; gerçek sporcu yüzü veya ünlü benzerliği olmamalı.
- Forma, araç, ekipman ve arena üzerinde marka, sponsor veya federasyon logosu bulunmamalı.
- Görselin içine skor, buton, menü, filigran ve açıklama metni yerleştirilmemeli.
- Yalnız prompt açıkça istediğinde tek, doğru yazılmış büyük başlık kullanılmalı.
- Renk paleti: gece laciverti `#07111B`, grafit `#111820`, buz mavisi `#79D4FF`,
  vurgu mercanı `#EF596C`, kırık beyaz `#F5F7FA`.
- Görsel dili: premium spor belgeseli, sinematik fakat gerçekçi, temiz yayın estetiği.

Ortak negatif prompt:

> low resolution, blurry subject, plastic skin, oversaturated colors, flat
> lighting, bad anatomy, malformed hands, extra fingers, extra limbs, duplicated
> athlete, duplicated equipment, warped ball, distorted vehicle, incorrect
> perspective, floating objects, random text, misspelled typography, numbers,
> scoreboard, user interface, website mockup, brand logo, sponsor logo, team
> crest, trademark, watermark, signature, QR code, identifiable celebrity,
> identifiable real athlete, copyrighted uniform, blood, injury, crowd faces in
> sharp focus, excessive lens flare, artificial HDR, cartoon, illustration

## 1. Kayak — kullanılan ana hero

> Create a premium ultra-wide cinematic website hero for XYZSKOR, a modern
> Turkish sports intelligence platform. The scene takes place at blue hour in a
> monumental alpine mountain range after fresh snowfall. A single dominant
> mountain peak occupies the center of the composition. Place the exact uppercase
> word “KAYAK” in enormous clean geometric white sans-serif letters behind the
> mountain, spanning almost the entire width of the frame. The mountain ridge and
> peak must naturally occlude the middle and lower portions of the letters, while
> every letter remains recognizable. This is environmental typography integrated
> into the landscape, not a floating title card.
>
> A completely fictional alpine skier appears in the lower-right third, carving
> aggressively from right to left through untouched powder. Show a physically
> accurate low stance, parallel skis, planted poles and a broad plume of fine snow
> particles catching the side light. The helmet, goggles and suit are matte black
> and deep graphite with only very subtle coral-red seam accents; no logos, no
> numbers and no recognizable uniform. Keep the skier smaller than the mountain
> so the environment remains monumental.
>
> Camera: full-frame cinema camera, 28 mm wide-angle lens, low elevated chase
> perspective, 1/1600 second frozen athlete with delicate directional motion in
> the powder, deep focus from skier to mountain, realistic atmospheric haze.
> Lighting: cold cyan ambient light, soft predawn sky gradient, restrained coral
> rim light from the far right, believable snow bounce, detailed but not crushed
> shadows. Color grade: dark navy, steel blue, graphite and clean white with one
> controlled coral accent. Leave the lower-left area calm enough for a translucent
> interface panel. Premium editorial sports photography, photorealistic,
> high dynamic range, realistic snow texture, cinematic depth, 16:9.

Kayak özel negatif ek:

> snowboard, ski resort advertising, ski lift dominating the scene, lodge,
> tourist crowd, avalanche, multiple skiers, crossed skis, malformed poles,
> impossible tracks, text other than the exact word KAYAK

Bu branş veri kapsamı yetersiz olduğu için üründen emekli edildi; görsel çıktısı
production assetleri arasından kaldırıldı. Bu metin yalnız tarihsel tasarım briefidir.

## 2. Motor sporları — hız ve veri merkezi

> Create an ultra-wide cinematic motorsport hero for XYZSKOR. Show a completely
> fictional open-wheel single-seat race car at maximum speed on a night circuit,
> captured from an extremely low rear three-quarter chase angle only one meter
> above the asphalt. The vehicle must have believable modern aerodynamics,
> suspension, slick tires, rear diffuser and halo structure, but must not reproduce
> any real Formula racing team, current livery or manufacturer design. Use a deep
> graphite body with narrow coral-red and ice-blue illuminated accents and no
> lettering, numbers, sponsors or logos.
>
> The car is razor sharp around the cockpit, rear tire and aerodynamic surfaces;
> the circuit barriers, road markings and distant lights stretch into controlled
> directional motion blur. Long red, amber and cyan light trails should lead from
> the left background toward the car and create a visual data-stream feeling.
> Suggest Istanbul only through an abstract distant suspension-bridge silhouette
> and dark waterfront lights—do not reproduce branded signage or a real event.
> Add a faint, technically accurate circuit telemetry motif in the atmosphere:
> thin grid lines and minimal luminous trajectory points, subtle enough to remain
> photographic rather than becoming a UI mockup.
>
> Camera: 24 mm anamorphic cinema lens, f/2.8, 1/80 panning exposure, crisp car and
> rotational tire blur, dramatic vanishing point. Lighting: wet asphalt reflections,
> cool moonlit fill, hot coral rear light and thin cyan edge light. Composition:
> car on the right 40 percent, open dark space on the left for title and series
> selection, safe central crop for mobile. Premium automotive campaign photography,
> physical realism, high micro-detail, restrained contrast, 16:9.

Motor sporları özel negatif ek:

> Ferrari, Mercedes, Red Bull, McLaren, Porsche, Formula 1 logo, real driver,
> real circuit branding, road car, extra wheels, fused suspension, oval tires,
> stationary vehicle, crash, fire, debris, copied racing livery

## 3. Basketbol — arena vitrini

> Create a premium basketball broadcast hero for XYZSKOR inside a vast dark indoor
> arena. A completely fictional elite basketball player is suspended at the peak
> of a powerful one-handed dunk, viewed from a low baseline angle. Preserve correct
> anatomy: one hand controlling the ball above the rim, the other balancing the
> body, natural shoulder rotation, correct finger count and physically believable
> legs. The athlete wears an unbranded deep navy uniform with a single coral side
> stripe, no name, number, crest or sponsor. The face remains partially shadowed
> and is not identifiable.
>
> Four intense white arena lights create a halo behind the athlete. Warm amber light
> reflects from the orange ball and polished hardwood while ice-blue fill separates
> the silhouette from the crowd. Transform subtle court arcs and shot-chart dots
> into abstract geometric lines receding into the dark background, suggesting data
> analysis without showing an interface. The rim, net and backboard must be
> structurally accurate. Render the audience only as soft atmospheric bokeh.
>
> Camera: 20 mm wide-angle lens close to floor level, f/2.8, 1/2000 second, athlete
> and ball tack sharp, faint motion in loose fabric only. Composition: action in
> the right-center, generous dark negative space on the left, key elements retained
> in a center mobile crop. Photorealistic sports advertising, cinematic contrast,
> realistic skin and fabric, dark navy/amber/coral palette, 16:9.

Basketbol özel negatif ek:

> NBA logo, recognizable player, recognizable jersey, team colors copied exactly,
> extra basketballs, bent rim, broken net, impossible dunk pose, oversized hands,
> visible crowd faces, scoreboard, jersey text or number

## 4. Voleybol — yükseliş anı

> Create an ultra-wide cinematic volleyball hero for XYZSKOR. Show a completely
> fictional professional volleyball attacker frozen at the highest point of a
> spike above a regulation net, body extended diagonally from lower left to upper
> right. The hitting arm is fully loaded with anatomically correct shoulder and
> wrist position; the ball sits just ahead of the open hand at the instant before
> contact. Two opposing blockers may appear only as dark, softly focused forearms
> beyond the net, with correct hands and no visible identities.
>
> The venue is a premium indoor arena with teal and ice-blue atmosphere, narrow
> coral-red rim lighting on the attacker, and clean white overhead beams. Let the
> net grid continue subtly into the background as an abstract visual system. Add
> a few sharp floating chalk-like particles and fabric motion for energy, while
> keeping the ball, athlete and top tape crisp. The uniform is matte graphite and
> deep teal, unbranded and numberless.
>
> Camera: 35 mm full-frame lens from slightly below net height, f/2.5, 1/2500
> second, compressed crowd bokeh, natural perspective. Composition: athlete in the
> right half, empty dark left side for interface text, face angled away from camera,
> mobile-safe center crop. Photorealistic editorial sports image, realistic muscle
> tension and fabric, sophisticated teal/navy/coral grade, 16:9.

Voleybol özel negatif ek:

> beach volleyball, sand, bikini, branded federation ball, incorrect net height,
> player intersecting net, extra fingers, duplicated ball, basketball, legible
> jersey text, national team uniform, recognizable athlete

## 5. Amerikan futbolu — stadyum tüneli

> Create a cinematic American football hero for XYZSKOR. A completely fictional
> wide receiver in full protective equipment emerges from a dark stadium tunnel
> into bright floodlights and cold atmospheric smoke. Capture the instant the
> athlete accelerates into the field: torso leaning forward, one cleat gripping
> the turf, the other lifting naturally, arms driving with believable sprint
> mechanics. The helmet, shoulder pads and dark navy uniform must be realistic but
> entirely generic, with no team crest, league mark, sponsor, name or number.
>
> Use electric ice-blue light on one side and a restrained coral rim light on the
> other, with white stadium lights forming depth behind the player. Fine turf and
> smoke particles catch the light. Suggest tactical intelligence through extremely
> subtle dotted route lines embedded in the field haze—not a visible playbook or
> interface. Keep crowd details indistinct and faces unrecognizable.
>
> Camera: 32 mm lens at waist height, f/2.0, 1/1600 second, sharp helmet texture,
> gloves and front cleat, gentle background motion. Composition: athlete in the
> right-center with large clean left-side negative space and a safe center crop.
> Premium sports campaign photography, powerful but controlled, navy/blue/coral
> grade, realistic materials, 16:9.

Amerikan futbolu özel negatif ek:

> NFL logo, Patriots, Seahawks, recognizable player, real team uniform, helmet
> decal, jersey number, football held incorrectly, missing face mask, extra arms,
> rugby uniform, violent collision, injury, celebratory text

## 6. UFC / MMA — kontrollü gerilim

> Create a sophisticated MMA event hero for XYZSKOR featuring two completely
> fictional combat athletes standing several meters apart inside an unbranded
> octagonal cage before a bout. Use restrained side profiles and silhouette-driven
> lighting rather than identifiable faces. Both athletes have anatomically correct
> guards, realistic hand wraps and plain dark performance shorts with no logos,
> flags, names or sponsor marks. Their posture communicates concentration and
> tension, not active violence.
>
> Split the lighting language: controlled coral-red rim light behind the athlete
> on the left and ice-blue rim light behind the athlete on the right, meeting in
> neutral white haze at the center. The cage mesh should be physically accurate
> but softly out of focus in the foreground. Add subtle airborne atmosphere and a
> faint circular overhead light structure. Leave the center-left region dark and
> clean for event information; do not add posters, fight-card text or belt imagery.
>
> Camera: 50 mm cinema lens at chest height, f/2.2, shallow but believable depth
> of field, detailed skin texture without glamour retouching, controlled highlights.
> Composition remains effective in both 16:9 desktop and centered 4:5 mobile crop.
> Premium combat-sports documentary photography, graphite/navy/coral/cyan palette.

MMA özel negatif ek:

> UFC logo, real fighter, celebrity likeness, championship belt, blood, cuts,
> bruises, knockout, active strike impact, aggressive gore, boxing ring ropes,
> duplicated fighter, malformed fists, poster typography

## 7. Buz hokeyi — buz parçacıkları

> Create an ultra-wide cinematic ice hockey hero for XYZSKOR. A completely
> fictional hockey player executes a sharp hockey stop while controlling a black
> puck, photographed from a very low ice-level angle. Show structurally accurate
> skates, stick grip, helmet, visor, gloves and protective equipment. The plain
> graphite jersey carries only a restrained ice-blue shoulder panel and coral seam
> accent; no crest, sponsor, name, number or national identity.
>
> A dense crescent of ice crystals sprays across the right side, individually
> illuminated by cold cyan arena lights. Keep the player's front skate, puck and
> lower stick blade tack sharp while allowing slight directional blur in the far
> boards. Suggest rink geometry with subtle blue and red lines leading toward the
> vanishing point. Crowd and boards remain dark and unbranded.
>
> Camera: 24 mm lens positioned centimeters above the ice, f/3.2, 1/2500 second,
> realistic reflections and blade marks. Composition: athlete on the right third,
> calm navy negative space on the left, important action protected in the central
> mobile crop. Photorealistic premium sports editorial, cold detailed atmosphere,
> navy/cyan/white with one restrained coral accent, 16:9.

Buz hokeyi özel negatif ek:

> NHL logo, recognizable player, real jersey, readable board advertising, extra
> stick, multiple pucks, malformed skates, field hockey, fighting, blood, broken
> visor, impossible ice reflection

## Model kullanım notları

- ImageGen: Promptu doğal dilde olduğu gibi verin; negatif bölümü sonuna
  “Avoid:” başlığıyla ekleyin.
- Midjourney: Ana promptun sonuna `--ar 16:9 --stylize 125 --chaos 4` eklenebilir.
- Flux/Krea: Ana promptu positive prompt, ortak ve branş negatiflerini negative
  prompt alanına yerleştirin; guidance değerini aşırı yükseltmeyin.
- Görselde yazı gerekiyorsa önce yazısız kompozisyon üretmek, ardından yalnızca
  tek kelimelik başlığı ayrı bir düzenleme turunda eklemek daha güvenilirdir.
- Seçilen görseli WebP'e çevirirken hero için kalite `85–90` aralığı yeterlidir.
